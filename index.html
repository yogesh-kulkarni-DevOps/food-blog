Vegetable Pasta

By Simple Home Recipes | March 16, 2026

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup chopped vegetables
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta for 8 minutes
  2. Sauté vegetables in olive oil
  3. Add pasta and mix well
  4. Season with salt and pepper

Classic Omelette

By Simple Home Recipes | March 16, 2026

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Salt
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Chopped onions

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs with salt
  2. Heat butter in a pan
  3. Add onions and pour eggs
  4. Cook for 2 minutes and fold

Grilled Chicken Salad

By Simple Home Recipes | March 16, 2026

Ingredients

  • 200g chicken breast
  • Mixed greens
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Grill chicken for 10 minutes
  2. Mix with greens
  3. Drizzle oil and lemon
